About Me

I design and validate control algorithms, including one that guides a magnetic bead along a path without touching it.

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Southern Illinois University, where I joined the program in Spring 2022. In my graduate research with Dr. Arash Komaee, I design advanced controllers for nonlinear systems, with a focus on gain scheduling.
